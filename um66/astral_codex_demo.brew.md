# Chapter One
In the void between empires, physics is a suggestion and ritual is an interface.

## Two flavors of rules text

{{note,science
### Science
Sensors resolve probability into data. Your ship can scan a region as an action and learn one of the following: atmosphere, heat signature, active comms, or mass shadow.
}}

{{note,mystic
### Magic
Aether currents carry intention. When you cast into open space, choose one: conceal a signature, warp a short distance, or bind a door with a glyph.
}}
